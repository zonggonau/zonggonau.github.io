## How to Switch Back or Try Other Themes:

### Switch to Original Theme (Purple-Blue):

In `index.html` line 20, change:

```html
<link rel="stylesheet" href="styles-dark.css" />
```

To:

```html
<link rel="stylesheet" href="styles.css" />
```

### Available Themes:

1. **styles.css** - Tech Purple-Blue (original)
2. **styles-dark.css** - Professional Dark (current) ⭐
